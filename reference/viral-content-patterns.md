# Viral Content Patterns — Amused

## The 8 Archetypes

### 1. Authentication Drama
"Is this REAL or FAKE?" — show a piece, build suspense, reveal
**Hook pattern:** "This [item] sold for [price]. Here's why I almost bought a fake."
**Best for:** Chanel, Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Gucci

### 2. Price Shock
Contrast retail vs resale price. Make the viewer feel smart.
**Hook pattern:** "She paid [retail]. I got the same one for [resale price]."
**Best for:** Birkins, flap bags, belt bags

### 3. Unboxing + Condition Reveal
The anticipation of opening. Show condition honestly — builds trust.
**Hook pattern:** "I spent [X] SAR on this preloved [item]. Here's what I got."

### 4. Styling / Outfit Build
Show how a single preloved piece elevates an outfit.
**Hook pattern:** "One [item] — 3 outfits you can wear in [city/occasion]"

### 5. Behind the Scenes: Sourcing
Show where the pieces come from — buying trips, storage, authentication process.
**Hook pattern:** "How I source [luxury brand] pieces for my shop"

### 6. Controversial/Contrarian
Challenge a common belief in the luxury space.
**Hook pattern:** "Buying new luxury in 2025 is a mistake. Here's why."

### 7. List / Ranking
"Top 5 preloved buys under [X] SAR" — digestible, shareable
**Hook pattern:** "5 luxury pieces that HOLD their value (buy preloved)"

### 8. GCC-Specific / Cultural Moment
Tie content to Ramadan, Eid, National Day, wedding season, summer travel
**Hook pattern:** "The best preloved bags for [Eid / wedding season / travel to Europe]"
