# Scripting Voice — Amused

## Tone
Confident, knowledgeable, girlfriend-energy. You're the friend who knows luxury.
NOT salesy. NOT corporate. Warm but direct.

## Language Style
- Mix Arabic and English naturally (code-switching is authentic to GCC audience)
- Use SAR for prices (not USD unless comparing international)
- Speak to her like she's smart — she knows luxury brands, don't over-explain

## Do
- Be specific: exact prices, exact model names, exact conditions
- Build tension before the reveal
- End with a clear call to action ("link in bio", "DM to shop", "comment X")
- Use "we" to make it feel like a community

## Don't
- Don't use generic luxury adjectives ("stunning", "gorgeous" overused)
- Don't be apologetic about preloved — celebrate it
- Don't overuse exclamation marks
- Don't pitch in the first 3 seconds — hook first

## Pacing (Short-Form)
- 0–3s: Hook — one strong statement or question
- 3–10s: Problem or context
- 10–25s: Reveal or demonstration
- 25–30s: CTA

## Signature Phrases
- "This is how smart shoppers do it."
- "Preloved doesn't mean less."
- "Authenticated. Delivered. Yours."
- (Arabic) "الفاخر ما يصير رخيص، يصير أذكى"
