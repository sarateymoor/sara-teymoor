# Audience Avatar — Amused

## Primary Buyer

**Name:** Nour (fictional)
**Age:** 26–38
**Location:** Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi
**Language:** Arabic-first, fluent English
**Income:** Upper-middle to high — disposable income for luxury

**Lifestyle:**
- Follows @chanel, @dior, @loewe on Instagram
- Shops Ounass, Farfetch, Vestiaire Collective
- Attends events: weddings, girls' brunches, business dinners
- Values: authenticity, status signaling, sustainability (secondhand = smart)
- Pain points: can't afford new luxury prices; worried about fakes; wants to refresh wardrobe constantly

**Content she responds to:**
- "Is this real?" authentication videos
- Before/after styling (how to wear it)
- Price comparisons (retail vs resale)
- Unboxing and condition reveals
- "What I got for X SAR / AED"
- Behind-the-scenes of sourcing / buying trips
- "You don't need to spend X when you can get Y"

**Platforms:**
- Instagram Stories + Reels (daily)
- TikTok (evenings)
- Pinterest (mood boarding)

## Secondary Audience: Sellers

**Age:** 28–45
**Goal:** Sell luxury items they no longer use, get fair price
**Content they respond to:** "How I sold my bag for X", "What sells fastest", "Tips for selling luxury"
