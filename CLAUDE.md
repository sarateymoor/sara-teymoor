## Content Criteria (TAM + Demo + Hook)

1. **TAM** — Does this resonate with GCC women aged 20–40 who buy or sell luxury?
2. **Demo-ability** — Can you show this on camera? (unboxing, authentication, before/after, styling)
3. **Hook Potential** — Does this fit a proven hook pattern?
4. **GCC Relevance** — Does this connect to Gulf culture, seasons, events (Ramadan, National Day, wedding season)?
5. **Timeliness** — Is this fresh (within 48 hours)?
